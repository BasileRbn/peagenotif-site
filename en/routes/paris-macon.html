Toll Paris → Mâcon: 2026 price

Between Paris and Mâcon, 2 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €34.50 to €35.30, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Mâcon → Paris). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.

Price by plaza

Mâcon has several toll plazas: the price depends on the one where you enter and the one where you leave. The table lists them all, cheapest first.

Paris has no toll plaza in its own name: the table stops its rate to Paris at the point “Paris (péage de Fleury-en-Bière)”. The price shown is the one for the trip to that point.
