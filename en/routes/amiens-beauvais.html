Toll Amiens → Beauvais: 2026 price

Between Amiens and Beauvais, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official Sanef table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €4.90 to €6.00, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Beauvais → Amiens). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
