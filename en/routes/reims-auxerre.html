Toll Reims → Auxerre: 2026 price

Between Reims and Auxerre, 8 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €27.60 to €29.50, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Auxerre → Reims). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
