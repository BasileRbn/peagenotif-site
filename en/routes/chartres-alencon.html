Toll Chartres → Alençon: 2026 price

Between Chartres and Alençon, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €16.40 to €17.90, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Alençon → Chartres). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
