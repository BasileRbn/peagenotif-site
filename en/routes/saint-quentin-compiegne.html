Toll Saint-Quentin → Compiègne: 2026 price

Between Saint-Quentin and Compiègne, 2 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official Sanef table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €7.50 to €8.00, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Compiègne → Saint-Quentin).
