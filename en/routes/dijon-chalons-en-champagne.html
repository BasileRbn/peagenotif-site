Toll Dijon → Châlons-en-Champagne: 2026 price

Between Dijon and Châlons-en-Champagne, 3 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €25.80 to €29.80, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Châlons-en-Champagne → Dijon). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
