Toll Orléans → Bourges: 2026 price

Between Orléans and Bourges, 2 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official Cofiroute table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €12.30 to €14.00, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Bourges → Orléans). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
