Toll Vierzon → Orléans: 2026 price

Between Vierzon and Orléans, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official Cofiroute table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €8.20 to €9.60, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Orléans → Vierzon). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
