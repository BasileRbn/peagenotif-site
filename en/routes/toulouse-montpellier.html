Toll Toulouse → Montpellier: 2026 price

Between Toulouse and Montpellier, 3 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €28.20 to €28.20, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Montpellier → Toulouse). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
