Privacy Policy

App Péage Notif — last updated: 31 July 2026
In one sentence: Péage Notif works with no account, no sign-up and no advertising tracker: all processing happens locally, on your iPhone. The only data that leaves the device is the error report you choose to send (see § 6) — never linked to your identity.

1. Data controller

The app is published by SECRETARAI, a SASU with capital of €50, registered with the Nice Trade Register under no. 103 272 100. Contact: contact@secretarai.fr — user support: support@peagenotif.fr.

2. No account, no profile, no tracking

Péage Notif works with no account and no sign-up. The app creates no user profile, uses no advertising identifier and sends no data for analytics, advertising or tracking purposes. The only data transmission is the error report you trigger yourself (described in § 6) — it contains no name, no email and no identifier of your device.

3. Location (GPS)

To detect that you are going through a toll, the app uses your device's position. This data is processed locally and in real time only, on your iPhone:

  • location is active only during a trip that you start manually (“Start tracking” button) and stops when you end the trip;
  • your position is not transmitted to the publisher or to any third-party server — with a single exception: the one-off positions contained in an error report you send voluntarily (§ 6);
  • the “While Using the App” permission is enough; the iOS location indicator stays visible during tracking.

4. Data stored on the device

Some information is saved on your iPhone only, for the app to work, and never leaves the device:

  • the history of your trips and the tolls you passed (estimated amounts, timestamps);
  • your preferences (vehicle class, voice announcement on or off);
  • an optional diagnostic log, which you alone can view, export or erase.

This data is deleted if you erase it in the app or if you uninstall the app.

5. Notifications, audio and Live Activity

Price notifications, voice announcements and the Live Activity (Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, CarPlay) are generated locally by the app. They rely on no remote notification service and transmit no data.

6. Error reporting (optional)

Nothing is transmitted until you have reported an error yourself (toll not detected, wrong price…): it is your tap on the report button that creates the report, and nothing else. Once the trip has ended, that report is then sent on its own, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), to a publisher's server hosted in Western Europe — so you have one less thing to do at the wheel. If there is no connection at that moment, it simply waits for the next time you open the app. No report is ever sent without you having triggered it, and you can erase at any time those that have not yet been transmitted, from Settings › Report an error.

This report contains only what is needed to correct the error:

  • the position, the speed and the timestamp at the moment of each report;
  • your dictated comment (transcribed to text) and any price correction you indicated;
  • the log of the trip concerned (timestamped detection events);
  • the version of the app and of the price list, and the configured vehicle class.

It contains no name, no email address, no phone number and no identifier of your device: we cannot link a report to a person. These reports are used solely to correct detection and prices, then deleted once the error has been dealt with. You can ask for a report to be deleted at support@peagenotif.fr stating the date and time it was sent.

7. Price updates

The price lists are built into the app. If an update check is available, it consists solely of downloading a public price list; no data concerning you is transmitted during that operation.

8. No data sharing or sale

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties, for any purpose whatsoever. Error reports (§ 6) are accessible to the publisher only and serve solely to improve the app.

9. Your rights (GDPR)

The data held on your device is under your sole control: you can view it, export it or delete it at any time from the app. The only data we hold are the error reports you have sent us (§ 6); they carry no direct identifier, and you can ask for them to be deleted at support@peagenotif.fr. For any question relating to data protection, write to contact@secretarai.fr. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).

10. Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children and knowingly collects no data concerning them.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top of the page shows the most recent revision. Substantial changes will be flagged on this page.
