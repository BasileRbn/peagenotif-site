Toll Besançon → Chalon-sur-Saône: 2026 price

Between Besançon and Chalon-sur-Saône, 9 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €11.20 to €11.80, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Chalon-sur-Saône → Besançon). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
