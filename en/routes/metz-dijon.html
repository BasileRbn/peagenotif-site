Toll Metz → Dijon: 2026 price

Between Metz and Dijon, 3 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €39.80 to €43.80, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Dijon → Metz). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
