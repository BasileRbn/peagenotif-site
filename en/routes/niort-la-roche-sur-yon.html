Toll Niort → La Roche-sur-Yon: 2026 price

Between Niort and La Roche-sur-Yon, 3 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €8.40 to €10.70, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (La Roche-sur-Yon → Niort). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
