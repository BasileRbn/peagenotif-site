Toll Reims → Château-Thierry: 2026 price

Between Reims and Château-Thierry, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official Sanef table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €4.60 to €6.90, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Château-Thierry → Reims). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
