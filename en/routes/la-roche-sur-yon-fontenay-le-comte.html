Toll La Roche-sur-Yon → Fontenay-le-Comte: 2026 price

Between La Roche-sur-Yon and Fontenay-le-Comte, 2 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €7.00 to €7.30, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Fontenay-le-Comte → La Roche-sur-Yon). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
