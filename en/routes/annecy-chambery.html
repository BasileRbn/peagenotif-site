Toll Annecy → Chambéry: 2026 price

Between Annecy and Chambéry, 2 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official AREA table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €5.60 to €5.90, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Chambéry → Annecy). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
