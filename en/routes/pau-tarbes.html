Toll Pau → Tarbes: 2026 price

Between Pau and Tarbes, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €3.10 to €4.60, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Tarbes → Pau). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.

Car · class 1€3.10 to €4.60
Motorcycle · class 5from €1.90
Heavy vehicle · class 4from €9.10
