Toll Arras → Saint-Quentin: 2026 price

Between Arras and Saint-Quentin, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official Sanef table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €5.40 to €7.00, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Saint-Quentin → Arras). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
