Toll Chalon-sur-Saône → Dole: 2026 price

Between Chalon-sur-Saône and Dole, 3 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €7.80 to €7.80, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Dole → Chalon-sur-Saône).
