Free-flow tolls

Tolls with no barrier: pay online in time, without a second thought

On the A13, A14 and A79, around Milan, at Dartford, on Dublin's M50 or in Portugal, gantries have replaced the barriers: this is free-flow tolling. Without a toll tag, how do you know whether you owe a toll, how much, and by when? Péage Notif tells you as you drive through.

PÉAGE
FLUX LIBRE

No barrier

You drive under the gantry without stopping. With a toll tag, the toll is charged automatically.

72 hto pay online

72 hours to pay online

Without a tag, you pay the toll online afterwards: 72 hours in France, a deadline that varies by country.

+10 € · +90 € · 375 €

Otherwise, a surcharge, then a fine

In France, the payment notice adds €10 if you settle it within 15 days, €90 after that. With no reply within two months, the fine rises to €375.

Support

A question? We answer.

Write to us at support@peagenotif.fr — or report an error straight from the app (Settings › Report a problem).

Which phones does Péage Notif work on?

On iPhone, from the App Store, and on Android, from Google Play. It is the same app on both: the price of every toll announced out loud as you pass, the deadline, reminder and calendar entry for free-flow tolls, and the end-of-trip summary.

Is the app really free?

Yes, entirely free and with no subscription. No account is required.

Are the prices shown accurate?

They are estimates calculated from the official price lists (class 1 by default, adjustable in settings). They are not binding: only the amount charged by your toll-badge operator or the motorway company is authoritative.

How do I find out the price of a toll?

As you drive through, Péage Notif reads it out and shows it, for your vehicle class. Before you set off, the app's “Trip price” button gives the fare between two toll stations, and this site lists the prices at every toll station.

Do I need a toll tag?

No. Péage Notif is independent of every operator and collects no tolls. It tells you the estimated price as you pass. On free-flow motorways (A13, A14, A79…), without a toll tag, you pay online afterwards: the app reminds you of the amount and the deadline.

Does the app drain my battery?

GPS only runs during tracking you have started. At rest the app uses nothing, and when you are stopped it slows the GPS down. If you forget to stop tracking when you arrive, it asks whether the trip is over.

A toll was not detected, or the price looks wrong

During the trip, tap the report button and say what is wrong. Afterwards, go to Settings › Report a problem, or write to support@peagenotif.fr. These reports go straight into correcting the toll database.

Which countries does the app work in?

In 18 countries: France and Italy across all their toll motorways, thirteen other European countries, Morocco, Tunisia and Canada (Ontario and Quebec). The coverage map shows every toll it recognises.

How do I know if I have a free-flow toll to pay?

With no barrier, nothing stops you as you drive through. Péage Notif announces each gantry you pass with its amount and the deadline, then lists it in the end-of-trip summary, with how to pay. With a toll tag, there is nothing to do: the toll is charged automatically.

How do I pay a free-flow toll?

With a toll tag, there is nothing to do: the crossing is charged automatically. Without one, you pay online on the operator's official website, using your number plate: sanef.com for the A13, the A14 and exit 36 of the A4, paiement.aliae.com for the A79, within 72 hours. Péage Notif announces the amount as you pass the gantry, reminds you of the deadline and opens the right website in one tap. The price of every gantry is in our guide to paying French tolls online.

I forgot to pay a free-flow toll: what happens?

In France, after 72 hours a payment notice arrives by post: the toll plus €10 if you settle it within 15 days, plus €90 after that. With no payment or appeal within two months, the increased fixed fine is €375. It is all explained in our guide to fines and scam texts.

I received a text message claiming an unpaid toll. Is it Péage Notif?

No. Péage Notif will never ask you to pay and sends neither texts nor emails. Fraudulent texts of this kind are circulating: do not tap their link. A free-flow toll is paid only on the operator's official website, for example sanef.com for the A13 and A14.
