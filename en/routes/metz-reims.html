Toll Metz → Reims: 2026 price

Between Metz and Reims, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official Sanef table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €17.00 to €19.60, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Reims → Metz). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
