Toll La Roche-sur-Yon → Châtellerault: 2026 price

Between La Roche-sur-Yon and Châtellerault, 2 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €20.00 to €20.80, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Châtellerault → La Roche-sur-Yon). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
