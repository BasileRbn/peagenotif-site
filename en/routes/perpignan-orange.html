Toll Perpignan → Orange: 2026 price

Between Perpignan and Orange, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €27.50 to €27.80, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Orange → Perpignan). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
