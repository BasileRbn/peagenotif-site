Toll Poitiers → Tours: 2026 price

Between Poitiers and Tours, 2 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €12.50 to €14.90, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Tours → Poitiers). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
