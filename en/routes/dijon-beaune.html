Toll Dijon → Beaune: 2026 price

Between Dijon and Beaune, 6 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €3.20 to €4.90, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Beaune → Dijon). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
