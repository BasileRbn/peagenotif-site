Toll Dijon → Dole: 2026 price

Between Dijon and Dole, 2 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €3.90 to €4.40, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Dole → Dijon). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
