Toll Nice → Antibes: 2026 price

Between Nice and Antibes, 5 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official Escota table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €1.70 to €3.20, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Antibes → Nice). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
