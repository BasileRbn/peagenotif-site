Toll Poitiers → Cholet: 2026 price

Between Poitiers and Cholet, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €16.90 to €18.20, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Cholet → Poitiers). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
