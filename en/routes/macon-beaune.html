Toll Mâcon → Beaune: 2026 price

Between Mâcon and Beaune, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €6.60 to €8.10, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Beaune → Mâcon). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
