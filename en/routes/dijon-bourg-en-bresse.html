Toll Dijon → Bourg-en-Bresse: 2026 price

Between Dijon and Bourg-en-Bresse, 6 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €12.90 to €14.80, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Bourg-en-Bresse → Dijon). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
