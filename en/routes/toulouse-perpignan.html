Toll Toulouse → Perpignan: 2026 price

Between Toulouse and Perpignan, 2 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €22.50 to €22.70, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Perpignan → Toulouse). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
