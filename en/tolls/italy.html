Italy: tolls, prices and how to pay

At every toll you pass, Péage Notif shows the price and reads it out loud, with no account and offline. Here are the essentials before you set off.

The essentials

  • Tolls recognised by the app in this country: 561
  • Toll barriers: you pay on the spot, as you go through.
  • Pedemontana: free-flow, no barrier. Without a toll tag, you pay for the crossing online afterwards on apl.pedemontana.com. Time limit: 15 days.
  • Corda Molle: free-flow, no barrier. Without a toll tag, you pay for the crossing online afterwards on autoviapadana.it. Time limit: 15 days.
  • Péage Notif reminds you of the deadline with a notification and adds it to your calendar in one tap.
  • Official prices from the operators, checked regularly. They are estimates: only the amount you are charged is authoritative.
