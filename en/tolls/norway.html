Norway: tolls, prices and how to pay

At every toll you pass, Péage Notif shows the price and reads it out loud, with no account and offline. Here are the essentials before you set off.

The essentials

  • Tolls recognised by the app in this country: 375
  • Free-flow tolls with no barrier: nothing to pay on the spot. The bill arrives in the weeks that follow. For a vehicle registered abroad, it is handled by epass24.com.
  • Currency: Norwegian Krone (NOK). In brackets, the euro equivalent at the European Central Bank rate of 8 October 2026.
  • Official prices from the operators, checked regularly. They are estimates: only the amount you are charged is authoritative.
