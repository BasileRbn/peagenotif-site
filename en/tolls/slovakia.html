Slovakia: compulsory vignette

In this country, cars pay to use the roads with a vignette bought in advance. Péage Notif reminds you out loud as you cross the border, with the official website where you can buy it.

The essentials

  • A vignette is compulsory on motorways: buy it on eznamka.sk before you use them.
