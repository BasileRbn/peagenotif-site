Toll Valence → Béziers: 2026 price

Between Valence and Béziers, 6 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official ASF table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €28.40 to €29.30, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Béziers → Valence). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
