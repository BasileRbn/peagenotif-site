Toll Mâcon → Villefranche-sur-Saône: 2026 price

Between Mâcon and Villefranche-sur-Saône, 4 combinations of toll plazas are listed in the official APRR table. For a car (class 1) the toll ranges from €2.90 to €6.90, depending on where you enter and where you leave. The price is the same in the other direction (Villefranche-sur-Saône → Mâcon). Rates in force since 1 February 2026. Amounts are estimates for guidance only: only the rate billed by your operator is binding.
